Some families live far away. Others cannot afford expensive care services. Everyone wants the same thing: for the people they love to be safe, and to feel close.

That's why we created hal.

But what happens in an emergency?

hal includes a connected watch with an emergency button. It detects falls and helps contact emergency services, transmitting important information such as the wearer's location.

How it works

Everything works together to make life safer.

  1. 01

    A simple tablet

    A standard tablet with our own operating system, specially designed to be simple and accessible.

  2. 02

    A connected watch

    Sensors detect falls and monitor skin temperature, with an emergency button always within reach.

  3. 03

    An assistant that helps

    The watch sends its data to the tablet, where our AI assistant analyzes it and gives useful recommendations.

  4. 04

    Always connected

    Internet access brings online services, weather, family communication and emergency assistance.
